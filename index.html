    Common questions

    PackAI converts your inputs (moisture, fat, pH, respiration, storage, shelf life…) into barrier & mechanical requirement vectors, then scores each material in the database across oxygen barrier, moisture barrier, light barrier, strength, scalability, flexibility, cost and sustainability. The ranked scores drive the recommendation.

    MAP replaces the air inside the pack with a gas blend (typically O₂, CO₂, N₂) to slow respiration, microbial growth and oxidation. PackAI suggests MAP for active-respiring fresh produce and recommends a gas composition along with breathable or micro-perforated films to balance O₂ ingress and CO₂ egress.

    Yes. The system is explicitly designed for small food industries, startups, farmers and local manufacturers. It factors cost and sustainability preferences and suggests practical, purchasable film types with target OTR/WVTR values you can request from suppliers.

    The forecast is a heuristics-based estimate derived from commodity base shelf life, packaging barrier performance, and storage conditions — ideal for comparing options and shortlisting materials. Real commercial shelf life requires validation trials per your exact product formulation.
